Laws of Sines and Cosines

To solve a triangle, geometric formulas like the Law of Sines: a/sin(A) = b/sin(B) = c/sin(C) or the Law of Cosines: c² = a² + b² - 2ab*cos(C) are used. Area is calculated using Heron's Formula for SSS inputs.
