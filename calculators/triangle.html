Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: What is the SSA triangle ambiguity?
A: When solving a triangle using Side-Side-Angle inputs, there can be zero, one, or two possible matching triangles depending on side lengths.

Q: What is Pythagoras' theorem?
A: In a right-angled triangle, the square of the hypotenuse is equal to the sum of the squares of the other two sides (a² + b² = c²).
