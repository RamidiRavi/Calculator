Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Why are two host addresses unusable in a subnet?
A: The first address (network address) and the last address (broadcast address) are reserved and cannot be assigned to hosts.

Q: What is a wildcard mask?
A: A wildcard mask is the inverse of the subnet mask, used in routing access control lists (ACLs) to match IP address ranges.
