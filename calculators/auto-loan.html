How is an Auto Loan Calculated?

Auto loan calculations are based on standard amortization formulas, but they incorporate unique variables such as down payments, trade-ins, and vehicle sales taxes.

The Formula

The net principal amount (P) is determined by subtracting your down payment and trade-in value from the vehicle purchase price: P = Price - DownPayment - TradeIn.

The monthly payment (M) is then calculated using the compounding amortization formula: M = P * (r(1+r)^n) / ((1+r)^n - 1), where r is the monthly interest rate and n is the loan term in months.
