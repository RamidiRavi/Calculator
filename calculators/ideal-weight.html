How Ideal Body Weight (IBW) is Estimated

Ideal Body Weight equations were originally developed to determine dosages for pharmaceutical products. Today, they are popular markers in physical therapy and training plans.

The Devine Formula (1974)

Devine's equation is the most widely referenced calculation algorithm:

  • Male: 50.0 kg + 2.3 kg per inch over 5 feet
  • Female: 45.5 kg + 2.3 kg per inch over 5 feet
