Strategic Retirement Planning

Planning for retirement requires understanding the real rate of return. A nominal 7% return feels great, but with a 2.5% inflation rate, the actual purchasing power of your money only grows by about 4.4% per year. This calculator models both to give you a clear estimate of your retirement portfolio's true size.
