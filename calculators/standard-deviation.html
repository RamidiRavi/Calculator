Sample vs. Population Standard Deviation

Sample Standard Deviation (s) divides the sum of squared differences by n - 1 (Bessel's correction) to prevent bias when analyzing small dataset samples.

Population Standard Deviation (σ) divides by the total count n, assuming the dataset contains the entire population.
