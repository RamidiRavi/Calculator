Key Financial Ratios

Gross Margin displays the percentage of revenue remaining after paying off direct production costs (COGS). It measures core product profitability.

Operating Margin represents the percentage of revenues remaining after accounting for operating overhead costs (rent, salaries, research). It highlights operational efficiency.
