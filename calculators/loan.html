Understanding Loan Calculations & Rates

A loan calculator helps you understand the true cost of debt. By breaking down your repayments, you can determine how different interest rates and loan terms affect your financial commitments.

Amortization Schedule Explained

Amortization is the process of spreading out a loan into a series of equal periodic payments. Although the total payment remains identical each month, the distribution between interest and principal shifts: at the beginning of the term, interest charges are high. As the principal drops, interest costs drop, allowing more of your payment to settle the core debt.

How to Save Money on Interest

  • Shorten the Term: Swapping from a 30-year term to a 15-year term results in slightly higher payments, but cuts total interest costs by over 50%.
  • Prepayments: Making extra payments directly to your principal reduces the balance faster, compounding interest savings over time.
