How Concrete Volume is Calculated

Concrete volume is determined by multiplying slab surface area by thickness: Volume = Length * Width * (Thickness / 12). The result in cubic feet is divided by 27 to obtain cubic yards. An 80 lb bag of concrete yields approximately 0.6 cubic feet.
