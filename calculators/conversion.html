Q: What is the metric system?
A: The metric system is a decimal-based system of measurement used globally, structured around powers of 10.

Q: How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?
A: Multiply the Celsius temperature by 9/5 (1.8) and add 32 (e.g. (100 * 1.8) + 32 = 212°F).
