Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: What is a cumulative GPA?
A: Cumulative GPA is the weighted average of all grades earned across your entire academic career at the institution.

Q: How does an F affect my GPA?
A: An F grade awards 0.0 points but still counts toward total credit hours, significantly lowering your GPA average.
