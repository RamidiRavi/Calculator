Unlocking the Secrets of Mathematical Inequalities

To begin, we apply the Arithmetic Mean-Geometric Mean (AM-GM) inequality, which states that the arithmetic mean of a collection of positive numbers is greater than or equal to their geometric mean. This leads us to establish that $\left( rac{a+b+c}{3} ight)^{2} \ge (abc)^{2/3}$. Utilizing this relationship helps in transforming the inequality we are analyzing and paves the way to expressing our variables in the context of one another.
