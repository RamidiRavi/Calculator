A Step-by-Step Guide to Confirming Real Roots of 2x³ + 9x² - 24

The cubic polynomial 2x³ + 9x² - 24 = 0 presents an intriguing challenge for mathematicians. Without delving into calculus, we can explore methods grounded in algebra to ascertain whether all roots of this polynomial are real.

Understanding the Nature of Roots

The quickest way to verify the reality of roots in a cubic polynomial is by examining the behavior of the polynomial itself. One effective approach is to leverage Vieta’s formulas, which connect the roots of the polynomial to its coefficients. For a cubic polynomial of the form ax³ + bx² + cx + d = 0, if it has real coefficients, any complex roots must appear in conjugate pairs. Therefore, confirming that all roots are real simplifies our problem considerably.

Applying Vieta's Formulas

Assuming the roots of our polynomial are a, b + ic, and b - ic (where i is the imaginary unit), Vieta's gives us three equations based on the relationships between the roots and coefficients:

  • a + 2b = -9/2
  • 2ab + b² + c² = 0
  • a(b² + c²) = 12

These equations posit restrictions on b and c. Particularly, if we let b = 0, we find c = 0, leading to a contradiction. Thus, this necessitates that b cannot be zero, and hence all roots must be real.

Conclusion: All Roots Are Real

This analysis establishes that each possibility examined leads us back to integers—indicative of the presence of real roots. Therefore, without the use of calculus, we have successfully demonstrated that all roots of the polynomial 2x³ + 9x² - 24 = 0 are indeed real.
