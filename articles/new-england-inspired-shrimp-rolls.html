Elevate Your Weeknight Dinner with Shrimp Rolls

If you're looking for a quick and satisfying meal, look no further than New England-inspired shrimp rolls. This dish offers the coastal charm of traditional lobster rolls but with plump, juicy shrimp, making it a fantastic seafood alternative that's equally delicious.

The recipe takes only 15 minutes from start to finish, which makes it ideal for busy weeknights. By blending mayonnaise and Greek yogurt, you'll get a creamy, tangy sauce that's lower in fat compared to traditional recipes, while still being rich in flavor. The addition of fresh green onions and lemon zest brightens the dish, making each bite feel like a breeze from the ocean.

Incorporating shrimp into your meals is not only convenient, it's also a smart dietary choice. A single serving provides 33 grams of protein and essential nutrients such as potassium and iron. For those keeping an eye on their calorie intake, one shrimp roll contains only 384 calories, making it a wholesome option for those looking to enjoy seafood without packing on the calories.

Serving the shrimp mixture in toasted split-top hot dog buns adds a comforting touch to this delightful dish. It’s simple, yet oh-so-satisfying, making it a great choice for gatherings or just a comforting dinner at home. So why not try this delicious recipe tonight and bring a taste of New England to your table?
