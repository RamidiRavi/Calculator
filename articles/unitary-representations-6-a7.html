Seeking Elegance in the 6-Dimensional Representation of 6.A7

The question of finding a nice unitary model for the faithful 6-dimensional complex representation of the Schur cover 6.A_7 is both intriguing and complex. This representation is essential in understanding the intricate structure of the group, yet existing characterizations, such as those provided by the ATLAS, present significant challenges regarding their unitarity under standard Hermitian forms.

Current generators, based on the ATLAS, are indeed exact and useful in an algebraic sense, particularly operating over the field K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{2},\zeta_3). However, the absence of unitary properties raises the question of how to achieve a representation that maintains both fidelity and simplicity. The prescriptive conjugation method towards standard unitary realizations often leads to complicated algebraic entries, prompting a search for more elegant alternatives.

A viable path forward involves examining the existing literature and mathematical frameworks surrounding 6.A_7. Look for known embeddings and geometrical constructions, as these might provide cleaner bases directly in SU(6). It's plausible that geometric representations could yield a natural orthonormal basis that aligns with the desired field constraints, thus eliminating some of the cumbersome algebraic tasks.

Furthermore, researchers actively engaged in group theory may have encountered similar challenges and devised effective solutions. Collaborating with mathematicians or consulting recent publications could illuminate pathways towards constructing unitary representations with simpler algebraic forms. It’s crucial to probe deeper into the existing studies and seek explicit references or examples of canonical constructions floating around in the discourse of representation theory.
