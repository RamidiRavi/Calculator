Nutritious and Delicious: One-Pot Chicken & Butternut Squash Stew

As the leaves begin to change and the air turns crisp, there’s nothing quite like a warm, hearty meal to comfort the soul. The One-Pot Chicken & Butternut Squash Stew is not only a breeze to prepare but also packed with nutrients that align perfectly with a balanced diet. Clocking in at just 319 calories per serving, this stew offers an array of flavors and a healthy helping of essential vitamins and minerals.

Why Choose This Stew?

Loaded with protein from boneless chicken breasts, and bursting with vitamin A from the butternut squash, this stew is not only filling but also beneficial for your overall health. It’s seasoned with ras el hanout, which introduces a unique flavor profile reminiscent of North African cuisine, enhancing your dining experience without compromising your health goals.

Quick and Easy Preparation

What makes this dish even more appealing is its efficient one-pot preparation, which means less time spent on dishes and more time enjoying your meal. You can whip it up in under 30 minutes—perfect for busy weeknights. The step-by-step guide ensures that anyone can recreate this flavorful stew, and substituting sweet potatoes for butternut squash adds even more versatility.

Nutritional Benefits

  • High Fiber: With 10g of dietary fiber, this stew supports digestive health.
  • Rich in Vitamins: Over 300% of your daily value for Vitamin A and a healthy dose of Vitamin C.
  • Low in Fat: At only 5g of total fat, it’s heart-healthy and satisfying.

As you indulge in this delicious dish, not only are you treating your taste buds, but you’re also nourishing your body. So grab your ingredients and enjoy a cozy fall evening with a bowl of this wholesome stew.
