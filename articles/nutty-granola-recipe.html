DIY Nutty Granola: A Nutritious Alternative to Store-Bought Snacks

If you’re looking for a healthy snack that satisfies your cravings without the guilt, homemade nutty granola might just be your new best friend. This simple recipe requires just six pantry staples: rolled oats, oil, honey, vanilla, cinnamon, and walnuts. In just 35 minutes, you can whip up a batch that serves 16, perfect for on-the-go breakfasts or afternoon nibbles.

Why Choose Homemade Granola?

Making your own granola allows you to control the ingredients, ensuring that you're not consuming unnecessary additives or refined sugars often found in store-bought options. The nutty granola recipe packs a powerful punch of nutrition, offering 305 calories per serving and a healthy mix of fats and fiber. Walnuts provide essential fatty acids, while rolled oats promote heart health and keep you full longer.

How to Make Nutty Granola

The process is straightforward. Start by preheating your oven and mixing your dry ingredients. Heat and whisk the wet ingredients together before combining them with the oats and nuts. Baking the mixture ensures it becomes crispy and golden, offering a delightful crunch that enhances your snacking experience. The granola can be stored in an airtight container for up to a month, making it a convenient option for meal prep.

Nutritional Benefits

A serving of this homemade nutty granola will provide you with more than just a delicious treat. With 6g of protein and 4g of dietary fiber in each serving, it contributes to your daily nutritional needs while keeping you energized. Plus, it features beneficial minerals like potassium and iron, further reinforcing its status as a health-conscious snack choice. So say goodbye to commercial granola and embrace the wholesome goodness of your own kitchen!
