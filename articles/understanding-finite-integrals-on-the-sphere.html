Is Your Integral on the Sphere Finite? Here's How to Find Out!

$I(\vec{n})= \int d\Omega'\, \dfrac{A(\vec{n}')-A(\vec{n})}{1- \vec{n}\cdot \vec{n}' }$

where $A(\vec{n})$ is a function devoid of poles. A common concern among scientists and mathematicians is the behavior of the integral when the vectors $\vec{n}$ and $\vec{n}'$ coincide (i.e., $\vec{n} = \vec{n}'$).
