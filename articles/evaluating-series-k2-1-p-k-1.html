Mastering Series Summation Techniques: A Deep Dive

The series in question, $\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} k^2 (1-p)^{k-1}$, is a fascinating example of how to engage with power series and their properties. When \( 0 < p \leq 1 \), this series converges and can be evaluated using some established mathematical techniques involving derivatives.

One effective method to evaluate this series is through the use of generating functions. By noting that the basic geometric series sums to $\sum_{k=0}^{\infty} x^k = rac{1}{1-x}$ for \( |x| < 1 \), we can differentiate this series to manipulate it into a form helpful for our purposes. Specifically, differentiating $\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} k x^{k-1}$ gives us another series that converges and reveals more about our target sum.
