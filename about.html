About OmniCalc

Our mission and development principles.

OmniCalc was founded with a single, clear objective: to build the fastest, most beautiful, and most private interactive calculators on the web.

In a digital world full of bulky websites, popups, and registration forms, we wanted to create a clean, responsive space where anyone can calculate everyday tasks without friction.

Our Development Principles

1. Privacy First

Most online calculators send your inputs to a remote server. We believe your personal data—especially your financial variables and biological details—should remain private. Every single calculator on OmniCalc operates entirely in your web browser (client-side). Your inputs are never stored, transmitted, or analyzed by us.

2. Performance & Speed

We do not use heavy javascript frameworks or bloated databases. OmniCalc is built on pure, optimized HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. This ensures that pages load in fractions of a second, even on slow mobile connections.

3. Accuracy & Simplicity

We spend hours verifying our mathematical formulas (such as amortization algorithms and the Harris-Benedict BMR formula) against official guidelines. We then package these formulas into simple, sliding controls that anyone can use instantly.

Who We Are

We are a small, independent team of developers, researchers, and designers passionate about web utility optimization. By building direct, focused tools, we help thousands of visitors make smarter everyday decisions regarding their health, money, and time.
